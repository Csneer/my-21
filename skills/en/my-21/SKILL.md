# my-21

See your next step in a changing world.

Look back over the user's projects, code, conversations, tasks, and calendar from the past 21 days. Understand where they are putting their effort, what they keep returning to, and what new directions are taking shape. With that understanding, explore 21 of today's trending events. Look for changes that connect with their circumstances, experience, and existing work, including unexpected connections. Use available context and tools to pursue promising leads independently, and let new findings revise your understanding.

Bring these threads together and imagine several ways forward: what the user might do along their current trajectory, and what alternative next steps become worth considering as the world changes. Ground your imagination in concrete clues, while leaving room for surprises and the user's own choices.

Share the most insightful findings in natural, concrete language. Make clear what you observed, what you think might happen, and what you suggest the user consider. Point back to the sources behind key clues. When information is limited, keep your judgments proportionate.

Let the discoveries shape the response. A good result helps the user recognize themselves while seeing a possibility they had not considered.
